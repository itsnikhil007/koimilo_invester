## 📌 Executive Summary

**Koimilo** is the culturally localized campus dating & social discovery network built specifically for India's 43 million college students across Tier-2 and Tier-3 educational hubs.

While foreign dating apps (Tinder, Bumble) bleed money and suffer from high female churn due to public hostel embarrassment, WhatsApp doxxing, and unaffordable ₹1,499/mo credit card paywalls, **Koimilo replaces cold swiping with psychological safety, double-blind confessions, in-app Agora VoIP audio calls, and 1-tap ₹49–₹99 UPI micro-sachets.**

## 🔑 Key Diligence Highlights

### 1. Ground Pilot Proof (Bhilai-Durg Cluster)
* **Ad Spend:** ₹1,000–₹2,000 lean Meta ad test targeting Bhilai college pin-codes (BIT Durg, Rungta, Padmanabhpur).
* **Inflow Spike:** Spiked to a peak of **16 verified students/day**; acquired **190 registered accounts**.
* **High Funnel Completion:** **140 out of 190 students (74%) completed full multi-step onboarding** (real photos, campus tags, confession prompts).
* **CAC:** Proven acquisition cost of **₹18–₹20**, over 20x lower than foreign dating giants.

### 2. The 4 Production Pricing Plans (100% UPI Micro-Sachets)
* **₹49 Weekend Pass (3 Days):** Impulse purchase for Friday–Sunday hostel banter and cafe meetups.
* **₹99 Weekly Sachet (7 Days • 72% of Sales):** Price of 2 canteen samosas; unlocks crush reveals & daily VoIP voice minutes.
* **₹149 Monthly VIP (30 Days):** Golden VIP spotlight placement on campus discovery feed.
* **₹399 Semester Pass (90 Days):** Full college term lock, maximizing upfront cashflow and eliminating exam-period churn.
* **Cashfree UPI Rail:** 1-tap checkout via PhonePe, GPay, Paytm with 0% App Store cut (direct PWA distribution), yielding an **88.2% contribution margin**.

### 3. Audited Unit Economics
* **Blended ARPU:** ₹149 / month
* **Estimated 3-Month LTV:** ₹447
* **Audited CAC:** ₹18.50
* **LTV : CAC Ratio:** **24.1x**
* **Net EBITDA Margin:** 90%+ at scale with low AWS/Supabase/Agora unit costs (< ₹8/user).

### 4. Cultural & Female Defense Moat
* **Agora In-App VoIP:** Female students voice-verify matches inside Koimilo without revealing phone numbers or Truecaller identity.
* **5-Chat Slot Cap:** Prevents male spamming; men must converse thoughtfully or forfeit the chat slot.
* **Double-Blind Secret Crush:** 100% elimination of public canteen/hostel rejection stigma.
